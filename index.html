Budget Program

A personal Windows app for reviewing spending, organizing receipts, and tracking a household budget.

How bank connections work

The owner uses Plaid to connect their own accounts and import transactions into the desktop app. They can then review and categorize purchases alongside their receipts.

Where the records live

The desktop app stores transaction history and receipt files on the owner's computer and backs them up to a second drive. This public page contains only information about the app.

Managing a connection

The owner can review connections shared through Plaid in the Plaid Portal.
